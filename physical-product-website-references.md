# Physical product website references for Hey Kahani

Researched October 28, 2026. These are new references for this project, focused on real physical products. Each link goes to a product-led site or a specific hero product page. The adoption and sales signals are linked separately.

## Start with these six

1. **[Light Phone III](https://www.thelightphone.com/)** — A restrained, editorial way to make an object and its purpose feel important. Study its singular product promise and physical details. [Fast Company reported more than 100,000 Light Phones sold](https://www.fastcompany.com/91549434/is-the-light-phone-getting-too-smart).
2. **[Hatch Restore 3](https://www.hatch.co/restore)** — A particularly relevant audio-led object. Study how the page sells a bedtime ritual through the clock, sound, light, routine, and bedroom context. Hatch reported [millions of sleepers and 75 million alarms in 2025](https://www.hatch.co/blog/hatch-tucked-in-2025).
3. **[Jolie Filtered Showerhead](https://jolieskinco.com/products/the-jolie-showerhead)** — A focused one-product story: show the object, explain a problem people recognize, demonstrate the solution, then resolve purchase doubts. Jolie reports [more than 775,000 customers](https://jolieskinco.com/pages/men-hair); [Shopify documented its eight-figure business](https://www.shopify.com/ph/blog/jolie-ecommerce-framework).
4. **[Clicks Keyboard](https://www.clicks.tech/en/pages/meet-clicks)** — Strong tactile product storytelling. Study its close-up object presentation, short feature sections, and language around the feeling of pressing a real button. Clicks reports [more than 200,000 keyboards sold](https://www.clicks.tech/pages/clicks-for-android).
5. **[WHOOP](https://www.whoop.com/)** — A premium hardware-led narrative connecting a small wearable to visible outcomes. Study the combination of product imagery, lifestyle context, and proof. WHOOP reported [more than three million members in 2026](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/press-center/whoop-names-dirk-jan-dj-van-hameren-chief-marketing-officer-as-member-base0/).
6. **[Flic Duo](https://flic.io/duo)** — A physical interaction explained through use cases. Study how it makes a simple press feel versatile; this is useful for showing how the Kahani Player and Koel cards work together. Flic reports [more than one million smart buttons shipped across its product line](https://cue.flic.io/). This figure is for Flic overall, not Duo alone.

## What translates best to the Hey Kahani homepage

- **Hero and product purpose:** Light Phone III, Clicks, Jolie.
- **Show the object in use:** Hatch Restore 3, Flic Duo, SNOO.
- **Explain Player + Koel in a few steps:** Tovala, Flic Duo, Litter-Robot.
- **Build parent confidence:** Hatch Restore 3, SNOO, Elvie Pump.
- **Give the brand more personality:** Jolie, Owala, TUSHY.

The strongest starting combination is **Light Phone III for restraint**, **Hatch for audio and ritual**, **Jolie for a focused product story**, and **Flic Duo for demonstrating a simple physical interaction**.
