# Hey Kahani — image generation identity

Hand this file to an image-generation agent. Goal: lifestyle photos with **Nodi-level warmth, color, and candid joy**, but **Hey Kahani product truth** and brand rules.

Reference mood (style only, not product shape): playful sunlit kid-tech lifestyle — textured quilts, patterned clothes, shallow depth of field, close emotional frames.  
Product truth: cylindrical **Kahani Player** + circular **Koel** discs (not a rectangular phone-like handheld).

---

## 1. Style bible (lock these)

### Mood
- Joyful, intimate, premium-but-homey kids audio
- Candid laughter / absorbed listening / quiet wonder
- “Homely yet aspirational” — real homes, not studio void
- South Asian diaspora family life as everyday, not costume

### Light
- Soft natural daylight or golden late-afternoon
- Gentle window light, soft shadows, slight warmth
- No harsh flash, no neon gels, no cold clinical LED

### Color grade
- Warm neutrals base: cream, sand, wood, soft white walls
- Brand accent: **Gulab Red `#D3000A`** only on the Player speaker band
- Textile/wardrobe may use marigold, sky, parrot green, lavender, mango — as fabric/art, not as product body colors
- Avoid muddy beige-everything; avoid rainbow oversaturation

### Texture & set dressing
- Loop rugs, quilts, knits, linen, wooden toys, crayons/paper when relevant
- Subtle cultural cues (block print, brass tray edge, marigold stems, picture book with South Asian art) — **never** garish “Bollywood costume” staging
- Keep clutter intentional and sparse enough that the Player/Koel stay readable

### Casting
- Kids ~3–8 (homepage core 3–6); South Asian / mixed diaspora faces preferred
- Parents only when the shot is about the companion-app / family moment
- Natural hair/clothes; patterned kidswear OK (like the Nodi ref energy)
- No celebrity lookalikes; no logos of other brands

### Lens language
- 35–50mm feel for scenes; 85mm feel for portraits
- Shallow depth of field; product edges stay sharp enough to read
- Hero/full-bleed: leave **negative space on one side** for website text overlay
- Avoid heavy vignette, heavy film grain, fake Instagram filters

---

## 2. Product lock (must match)

### Kahani Player — always
- Cream/off-white **cylindrical** body, rounded top
- Arch **carry handle** on top
- **Gulab Red woven speaker band** mid-body
- Light **wood-toned base**
- Side controls: plus, previous / play-pause / next, minus (simple embossed icons)
- Top: recessed circular plate for a Koel; optional small cyan light ring
- Matte plastic + fabric mesh + wood — tactile, premium toy-adjacent, not glossy phone

### Kahani Koel — always
- Perfect **circle** disc / “vinyl” story token
- Storybook illustration on the face (textured, warm, South Asian scenes)
- Can sit on Player top or lie nearby on rug/table
- Never a rectangular card like Yoto Card unless explicitly asked later

### Hard product don’ts
- Do **not** invent a rectangular Flip-like handheld as the Hero product
- Do **not** recolor the whole Player red / purple / orange
- Do **not** put a phone or tablet in the child’s hands during listening scenes
- Do **not** bake random art onto an empty Player top when no Koel is present (blank plate OK)
- Do **not** add Spotify / third-party logos or UI chrome in-frame
