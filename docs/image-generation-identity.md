# Hey Kahani image generation companion

Use the [photography style guide](photography-style-guide.md) for every new
lifestyle photograph. The direction approved on November 20, 2026 is Indian
children, candid everyday moments, warm light and playful colours.

The guide is the current source for casting, expression, light, colour,
composition, product identity, reusable prompts and image selection.

## Build a brief

1. Choose one everyday activity and the emotion within it. Specify the people,
   a few relevant objects, camera position and intended crop.
2. Attach an accurate product reference and one or two approved direction
   samples. Label each input as product identity or photographic style.
3. Use the guide's [master brief](photography-style-guide.md#reusable-generation-brief),
   then add a [scene starter](photography-style-guide.md#scene-starters) or a new
   scene in the same language.
4. Check the child's gesture and expression, natural skin, light, colour and
   product accuracy. Follow the guide's
   [selection and delivery guidance](photography-style-guide.md#selection-and-delivery).

## Reference inputs

| Input | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [Manufacturing angle](product-reference/manufacturing-front-angle.png) and [front view](product-reference/manufacturing-front.png) | Player geometry and controls; the grey sample speaker band becomes Gulab Red #D3000A |
| [Production-colour concept](../frontend/assets/images/player-production-v2.png) | Cream housing, Gulab fabric band and wood base; a generated concept recreation |
| [Four approved direction samples](photography-style-guide.md#visual-references) | Light, texture, candid expression, everyday activity and composition |
| Actual supplied Koel artwork, when available | Printed disc identity; generated sample illustrations do not establish published products |

Keep the Player cylindrical with its arch handle, five controls and wood base.
Koels are circular discs placed flat on top. Style inputs must not override
the product references or introduce rectangular cards and competitor devices.
