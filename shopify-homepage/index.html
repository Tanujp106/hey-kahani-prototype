We started for our kids.

We’re South Asian parents raising kids in the diaspora too. As a 4th generation Gujarati-Kenyan married to a 1st generation Tamil-American, we wanted our daughters to feel close to every part of where they come from. The stories we found never quite fit.

We started Hey Kahani because our kids deserve stories that feel like them. Full of our values, festivals, heroes, and humour. Not a watered-down version of culture. The real thing.

Come keep those stories close with us, for the next generation of diaspora kids.

Small questions. Straight answers.

What age is Hey Kahani for?

Hey Kahani is made for kids around ages 3 to 6, with stories that grow alongside them. Younger ones can listen too, with a grown-up nearby.

What languages do you support?

Every Kahani launches in English first. More stories will come in Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Urdu, and Bengali, so the words and names feel familiar.

Do kids need a phone or tablet to listen?

No. Kids listen on the Player. Grown-ups use the companion app for setup and guidance. For them, the story stays screen free.

How do Koels work?

Each Koel is a round story disc. Kids spot the artwork, place it on the Player, and the story begins. No menus. No fuss.

What’s in the box?

You’ll get the Kahani Player and what you need to start listening, plus a way into the growing Koel library. Exact kit details sit on the live product listing at checkout.
