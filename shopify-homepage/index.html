From our founders

We started for our kids.

We’re South Asian parents raising children in the diaspora too. As a 4th generation Gujarati-Kenyan married to a 1st generation Tamil-American, we wanted our daughters to feel connected to every part of their heritage. The stories we found were never quite the right fit.

We started Hey Kahani because our kids deserve stories that feel like them. Full of our values, festivals, heroes, and humour. Not a simplified version of culture. The real thing.

Join us in keeping those stories close for the next generation of diaspora kids.

With love,
The Hey Kahani founders

Little questions. Clear answers.

What age is Hey Kahani for?

Hey Kahani is made for little listeners around ages 3 to 6, with stories that grow with them. Younger children can listen too with a grown-up nearby.

What languages do you support?

Every Kahani launches in English first. Upcoming stories will include Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Urdu, and Bengali, so familiar words and names can feel like home.

Do kids need a phone or tablet to listen?

No. Kids listen on the Player. Grown-ups use the companion app for setup and guidance. The story stays screen free for them.

How do Koels work?

Each Koel is a circular story disc. Kids recognise the artwork, place it on the Player, and listening begins. No menus. No fuss.

What’s in the box?

You’ll get the Kahani Player and everything needed to start listening, plus a way into the growing Koel library. Exact kit details follow the live product listing at checkout.
