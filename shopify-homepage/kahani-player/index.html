Small questions. Straight answers.

What is the Kahani Player?

A screen-free audio player for South Asian stories. Children choose a circular Koel story disc, place it on top, and listen.

What age is Hey Kahani for?

Hey Kahani is made for kids around ages 3 to 6. Younger children can listen with a grown-up nearby.

Do kids need a phone or tablet to listen?

No. Kids listen on the Player. Grown-ups use the companion app for setup and guidance. For them, the story stays screen free.

How do Koels work?

Each Koel is a round story disc. Children spot the artwork, place it on the Player, and the story begins. Koels need a Kahani Player.

What comes with my Player?

The Kahani Player is the listening device. Included Koels and other box contents depend on the product or bundle you choose. Final pack contents will be listed before purchase.

Where can I find the hardware specifications?

The physical controls, carry handle and Player appearance are shown above. Battery, charging, connectivity, dimensions and weight details are still to be confirmed.
