A note from our founders

We’re South Asian parents raising children in the diaspora too.

As a 4th generation Gujarati-Kenyan married to a 1st generation Tamil-American, we dreamed of giving our daughters a deep connection to every part of their heritage, but the stories we found were never the right fit.

We started Hey Kahani because our kids deserve stories that feel like them—full of our values, festivals, heroes, and humor. Not a simplified version of culture. The real thing.

Join us in preserving our culture and traditions for the next generation of diaspora kids.

With love,

The founders
