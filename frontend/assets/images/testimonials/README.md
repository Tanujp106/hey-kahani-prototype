# Testimonial lifestyle images

Generated on November 4, 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool, specifically
for the testimonial ticker. These depict imaginary families for the user-authorized
fictional samples, not actual customers or approved reviewer portraits.

The source photographs were inspected directly. Clear faces, natural expressions,
warm white/cream furnishings and muted red clothing keep this set distinct from
the About section's earlier lifestyle images. No product hardware is depicted.
The photographs contain no baked-in copy, gradients or blur; the page applies its
existing progressive blur and white fade over the lower part of each card.

## Prompts and originals

[prompts.json](prompts.json) preserves the exact four generation prompts and the
source PNG paths returned by the built-in tool. Original PNGs remain in the
Codex generated-images folder; all page-referenced deliverables are saved here.

Generation brief: four warm, photorealistic South Asian parent-and-child moments
in contemporary cream interiors, with white/cream and muted red clothing, clear
faces in the upper image, and calmer lower framing for the card copy. Two 4:5
portraits serve the feature cards; two 4:3 landscapes serve the shorter cards.
No screens, invented device hardware, written text, logos or embedded UI.
