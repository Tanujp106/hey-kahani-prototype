# Hey Kahani website redesign references

Research date: 25 October 2026

This is a reference library for the planned Shopify redesign, based on the supplied Website Build SOW, Hey Kahani's public website, official reference sites, and previews returned by Mobbin. The SOW is treated as project context, not as an instruction to begin implementation. Links below point to the specific page or Mobbin capture worth studying. Public website findings are content and structure observations; no interactive browser or visual QA was performed.

## The product and the design problem

Hey Kahani sells a **screen-free audio Player** for children aged roughly 3–6 and illustrated **Koel cards** that trigger stories. Its point of difference is South Asian cultural connection: mythology, folklore, festivals, languages, and everyday diaspora childhood. The parent app handles setup and parent-facing features; the website needs to explain the physical system, sell the Player and additional Koels, and make the stories discoverable. The [current homepage](https://www.heykahani.com/), [Player page](https://www.heykahani.com/kahani-player), [Koel listing](https://www.heykahani.com/koels-listing), and [app page](https://www.heykahani.com/kahani-app) establish that public positioning.

The SOW makes these the key reference criteria:

- **Two buying journeys:** a first-time parent learning how Player + Koel works, and an existing owner finding the next Koel.
- **Story-first discovery:** browse by category, language, and series; show series/volume order, story lists, age guidance, and audio samples.
- **Clear commerce:** distinguish a physical Koel from its included individual stories, explain the Founder's Bundle, and keep Player, Koel, cart, and checkout paths simple.
- **Consistent public samples:** one reusable, accessible player across homepage, series, Koel, and eligible story placements, with play/pause, seeking, time, and clear unavailable states; samples must not autoplay.
- **Brand expression:** premium, warm, contemporary, illustration-led, culturally specific without clichés; retain Hey Kahani's brand guide, Averia Serif Libre, Open Sans, and approved product/art assets.
- **Practical constraint:** the eventual storefront is Shopify Online Store 2.0 with editable catalog/content sections. References are for ideas, not templates to copy.

**Content to reconcile before designing:** the [live Player page](https://www.heykahani.com/kahani-player) and [Koel listing](https://www.heykahani.com/koels-listing) currently say the $149 Starter Kit includes **20 Koels**; the SOW describes a Founder's Bundle with **two physical Koels containing 20 individual stories**. The [live Koel listing](https://www.heykahani.com/koels-listing) also presents a single story, “Hanuman Remembers,” as a $0 item alongside Koel products. Treat the SOW's physical Koel versus story distinction as the design requirement and confirm final merchandising copy with the client. The [app page](https://www.heykahani.com/kahani-app) mentions “subscription control,” while the SOW calls for no recurring subscription in the storefront message; confirm what this app copy refers to before reusing it.

## 1. Direct device and content competitors

These companies sell a child-friendly audio device and a growing library of compatible physical or digital content. Yoto and Tonies are named quality benchmarks in the SOW.

| Site | Why it belongs | Specific pattern to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Yoto](https://us.yotoplay.com/) | Card-based screen-free player plus a large content library. | The homepage separates player education from content discovery; the [card catalog](https://us.yotoplay.com/collections/library) and [card detail](https://us.yotoplay.com/products/space-science) show preview, age/language/runtime information, and track lists. This is the closest model for Koel discovery. |
| [Tonies](https://us.tonies.com/) | Physical audio characters paired with a player. | Study device versus content navigation and the [Tonie detail page](https://us.tonies.com/products/bedtime-songs-lullabies-tonie) for sample audio, age, duration, and track-list placement. Its figurine model differs from Koel cards. |
| [Storypod, now Eddi](https://www.storypod.com/) | Screen-free player with physical Crafties, books, and tokens; the site says Storypod is now Eddi. | The [Craftie Fox detail page](https://www.storypod.com/products/craftie-fox) pairs product imagery, a sample, and compatible-player reassurance. Useful for explaining what a Koel unlocks. |
| [FABA](https://www.myfaba.it/) | Storytelling device with physical audio characters and discs. | Its shop offers browsing by age, language, content type, and theme. Adapt the taxonomy idea to Hey Kahani's real, populated fields; the site itself is in Italian. |
| [Lunii](https://lunii.com/en-us/boutique) | Audio storyteller and purchasable story catalog. | Study the distinction between the device shop and [audiobook themes/ages](https://lunii.com/en-us/boutique). Its interactive-story model is less directly comparable than Yoto or Tonies. |

## What the references suggest for the redesign

1. Make the physical system legible in one glance: **Player → Koel → story**, then offer separate “Shop the Player” and “Explore Stories” paths.
2. Treat each Koel as both a purchasable product and a small story collection. The detail page should carry artwork, series/volume position, language, age, duration, a public sample, and the included-story list.
3. Use illustrated series cards as the discovery layer, then progressively reveal practical shopping facts. Make filters reflect content that actually exists.
4. Let the site feel rooted in specific family and story details; use approved Hey Kahani artwork and imagery instead of adopting another brand's illustration style.
5. Keep optional inspiration separate from scope: subscriptions, headless apps, complex personalization, and a large media-player interface are features of some references, not SOW requirements.
