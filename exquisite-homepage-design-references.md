# Exquisite homepage design references for Hey Kahani

Research date: 26 October 2026  
Purpose: a **visual quality bar** for the Hey Kahani homepage. These are design references, not a competitor list. The existing [homepage reference brief](homepage-redesign-references.md) and [broader reference library](website-redesign-references.md) remain separate.

Hey Kahani sells a physical screen-free audio Player and illustrated Koel story cards for young children. The homepage still needs to make **Player → Koel → story** clear, offer Shop and Explore Stories paths, feature a playable sample, and explain the bundle. The sites below suggest ways to make that experience feel unusually crafted. The official links are the sites to open; gallery and studio links provide design context and may show older versions. This research used public web pages and design showcases, without interactive browser or viewport review.

| Site | Why it belongs in the first review | Most useful Hey Kahani section |

## Children's products: high craft, different moods

### 1. [Blok Watches](https://blokwatches.com/) — product explanation as the main event

Blok sells analog watches for children. Its homepage invites a visitor to set a time interval and see how the watch works before moving into product details. The interaction makes a novel physical feature understandable and gives the hero a reason to exist beyond a beautiful image. For Hey Kahani, a similarly concise **Koel touches Player → a story begins** demonstration could make the device feel instantly graspable. Study the relationship between the demo, product image, and shop action. [Design/build case study](https://www.datocms.com/partners/antinomy-studio/showcase/blok-watches) · [Webby visual-design recognition](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2023/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-visual-design-aesthetic/249436/blok-watches)

### 2. [Woset](https://woset.world/) — imagination without a cluttered storefront

Woset sells creative tools for children. Its sparse navigation and product presentation leave room for unusual names, characters, and colorful objects to carry the personality. Study how a small number of consistent visual rules make a product catalog feel like a world. For Hey Kahani, this is a useful direction for illustrated Koel cards and story collections: art can lead, while title, category, and purchase path stay easy to read. [Siteinspire feature](https://www.siteinspire.com/website/12311-woset) · [Ecommerce Design Awards feature](https://www.ecomdesignawards.com/websites/woset-world)

### 3. [Wild Thingz](https://wildthingz.co.uk/) — a character world with real energy

Wild Thingz sells lower-sugar sweets to children and their parents. Its site and identity use a mascot, graphic typography, and a distinctive visual setting to make the product recognizable. For Hey Kahani, study the **consistency** of the world-building: characters, backgrounds, packaging, and sections all feel related. Its punk mood is much sharper than Hey Kahani's warm storytelling tone, so the useful lesson is the coherence of the system, not the palette or attitude. [Design studio case study](https://www.thedca.co/work/wild-thingz) · [Siteinspire feature](https://www.siteinspire.com/website/12792-wild-thingz)

### 4. [Coterie](https://www.coterie.com/) — premium parent-facing commerce

Coterie brings the visual care usually associated with luxury products to baby care. Its design work spans photography, motion, product explanation, and shopping. Study how parent-facing reassurance sits beside a polished product presentation. Hey Kahani can use this as a benchmark for the Player and Founder's Bundle: clear product imagery, specific contents, and a confident purchase action within a warm premium page. [Design and development case study](https://numbered.com/project/coterie) · [Ecommerce Design Awards feature](https://www.ecomdesignawards.com/websites/coterie)

### 5. [Paloroma](https://paloroma.com/) — quiet luxury for a small children's range

Paloroma sells baby and children's skincare with a compact assortment. The homepage gives individual essentials and gift sets enough room to feel considered. Study its restraint and the way a small catalog is merchandised without forcing extra sections. For Hey Kahani, this is a useful reference for presenting the Player, two included Koels, and a starter bundle as a complete set. [Siteinspire feature](https://www.siteinspire.com/website/9661-paloroma) · [Ecommerce Design Awards kids collection](https://www.ecomdesignawards.com/category/kids)

## Outside the niche: exceptional brand and product worlds

### 6. [Vacation](https://vacation.inc/) — a fully realized brand premise

Vacation sells sunscreen through a deliberately nostalgic leisure world. The homepage mixes a strong campaign premise, staged product imagery, editorial brand sections, and shopping routes. Study how the same idea carries from hero through product, proof, and story. For Hey Kahani, the equivalent is a recognizable **storytime world** that makes the Player and Koels feel part of a ritual in a family's home. [Ecommerce Design Awards feature](https://www.ecomdesignawards.com/websites/vacation)

### 7. [Le Rub](https://lerub.com/) — contemporary editorial product photography

Le Rub sells sun care with clean product photography, a compact product lineup, and a strong editorial rhythm. Study the balance between large image moments, short copy, and immediate shopping. For Hey Kahani, borrow the discipline of giving the physical Player and bundle strong photographic space; keep illustrated Koel art as a complementary layer. [Ecommerce Design Awards feature](https://www.ecomdesignawards.com/websites/le-rub)

### 8. [Ghia](https://drinkghia.com/) — sensory commerce and clear starting points

Ghia sells nonalcoholic apéritifs. The site offers a discovery set, individual products, and a brand story in a distinctive visual language. Study how a visitor can understand the main product, pick an entry point, and then explore more deeply. For Hey Kahani, the Founder's Bundle can play the role of the obvious starting point, with individual Koels and story discovery following it. [Website design gallery](https://www.lapa.ninja/post/drinkghia/)

### 9. [D.S. & Durga](https://www.dsanddurga.com/) — products treated as stories

D.S. & Durga sells fragrances through named concepts, evocative descriptions, and a visible founder perspective. Study how product language and editorial imagery give each item its own narrative while the store remains shoppable. For Hey Kahani, this is a useful model for giving each Koel a clear identity and inviting people into its stories beyond a generic product card. [Siteinspire feature](https://www.siteinspire.com/website/10970-ds-and-durga) · [Design showcase](https://www.siteofsites.co/websites/d.s.-%26-durga)

### 10. [Omsom](https://omsom.com/) — cultural identity with an unmistakable voice

Omsom is an Asian food brand whose public site foregrounds its founders, cultural roots, and energetic brand language. Study how cultural specificity can shape typography, copy, and the whole visual voice instead of appearing only in an About section. For Hey Kahani, the transferable idea is to let its own South Asian storytelling roots influence the homepage's visual storytelling and founder section. Omsom's current public homepage is more brand and retail focused than a standard online store; use it for identity and storytelling direction. [Siteinspire feature](https://www.siteinspire.com/website/9518-omsom)

## What to carry into the Hey Kahani homepage

| Homepage need | Strongest references | Direction to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A striking opening that explains the object | [Blok](https://blokwatches.com/), [Coterie](https://www.coterie.com/) | One strong Player image or interaction, a brief promise, and clear Shop / Explore Stories actions. |
| Koels that feel like a story universe | [Woset](https://woset.world/), [Wild Thingz](https://wildthingz.co.uk/), [D.S. & Durga](https://www.dsanddurga.com/) | Give each collection a distinct visual identity within one coherent brand system. |
| A premium starter bundle | [Paloroma](https://paloroma.com/), [Ghia](https://drinkghia.com/), [Le Rub](https://lerub.com/) | Stage the real contents clearly and treat the bundle as the natural first purchase. |
| A memorable, culturally specific brand world | [Omsom](https://omsom.com/), [Vacation](https://vacation.inc/) | Build a consistent storytime mood and use Hey Kahani's own founder and cultural material. |

**Suggested visual mix:** take Blok's clarity of demonstration, Woset's imaginative catalog, Coterie's parent-facing polish, and Omsom's confidence in its own cultural voice. Use Hey Kahani's approved illustrations, product imagery, and stated typefaces to make the result original.
