# Hey Kahani homepage redesign references

Research date: 26 October 2026  
Scope: homepage only. The broader [website reference library](website-redesign-references.md) remains separate and unchanged.

This brief uses the supplied [Website Build SOW](</Users/tanuj/Downloads/Hey Kahani - Website Build SOW.docx>) as the requirement source, the [current Hey Kahani homepage](https://www.heykahani.com/) as the content baseline, official reference sites for their public page structure, and selected Mobbin captures for visible section patterns. No interactive browser or viewport review was performed.

## What the homepage has to accomplish

Within the first few sections, a new parent should understand **what the Player is, what a Koel is, why these stories matter, and how to buy the starter bundle**. An existing owner should see an immediate path into **Explore Stories** and additional Koels. The SOW's preferred homepage order is:

1. Announcement bar and navigation.
2. Product-led hero with **Shop the Player** and **Explore Stories** actions.
3. Illustrated **Explore Our Stories** cards near the top.
4. A playable **Listen to a Kahani** sample.
5. The **Founder's Bundle** with its contents and live Shopify price.
6. **How It Works** in three steps.
7. Featured Koels and new releases.
8. Why Hey Kahani, approved social proof, founder story, FAQs, and footer.

The homepage should feel warm, premium, contemporary, and illustration-led while using actual Player imagery and approved Koel artwork. The SOW specifies Averia Serif Libre for headings and Open Sans for body/interface text. References below are patterns to study, not visual styles or copy to import.

## Current homepage baseline

The [live homepage](https://www.heykahani.com/) already communicates South Asian stories, screen-free listening, a Player/Koel pairing, two shop/discovery calls to action, a founder story, newsletter, and FAQs. Its public text presents “No screens, no apps, no fuss,” while the [companion-app page](https://www.heykahani.com/kahani-app) describes parent-facing setup and listening features. In the redesign, the child experience can be described as screen-free while the parent's optional or necessary app role is explained accurately.

The public homepage text does not make a playable audio sample or an itemized two-Koel bundle explanation evident. These are explicit SOW homepage requirements. The [live Player page](https://www.heykahani.com/kahani-player) currently says the kit contains 20 Koels; the SOW says **two physical Koels and 20 stories**. Resolve that copy before designing the bundle section, and source price/availability from Shopify rather than the current public text.

## Homepage reference shortlist

| Reference | Type | Homepage pattern worth studying | Use for Hey Kahani |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Yoto](https://us.yotoplay.com/) | Direct device competitor | The homepage introduces the card-to-player action, then offers separate routes into players and the card library. It also uses age-based content entry points. | Make **Player → Koel → story** instantly understandable while preserving two paths for new buyers and existing owners. |
| [Tonies](https://us.tonies.com/) | Direct device competitor | Separates the box from the audio characters, then browses content by listening occasion and age. | Present the Player and Koels as connected but distinct purchases; borrow the content-discovery logic without the site's heavy promotional density. |
| [Storypod / Eddi](https://www.storypod.com/) | Direct device competitor | Introduces the player, then the content universe, with separate shop routes and simple reassurance about the system. | Explain the ecosystem in a short sequence before asking visitors to choose a purchase. |
| [Lovevery](https://lovevery.com/) | Premium parent-commerce reference | Product-led hero, age entry points, kit contents, and parent-facing proof. | Show the Player clearly, give parents a confident next action, and make the bundle feel complete rather than mysterious. |
| [Wonderbly](https://www.wonderbly.com/) | Illustrated story-commerce reference | Story-cover merchandising and browsing by child/occasion. | Let approved Koel artwork carry the **Explore Our Stories** section, as book covers do for a bookshop. |
| [Slumberkins](https://slumberkins.com/) | Connected product/story reference | A numbered explanation connects books, plush objects, and affirmations. | Make **Choose a Koel → Tap the Player → Listen and imagine** a real demonstration of the system, not three generic benefit cards. |
| [Modi Toys](https://moditoys.com/) | South Asian cultural-commerce reference | Cultural product promise, category shopping, and a short founder origin on the same homepage. | Pair specific family/cultural meaning with a clear shop path; use Hey Kahani's own family story and imagery. |
| [Pinna](https://pinna.fm/) | Children's audio-content reference | Story cover art and free sampling communicate the value of audio before a paid decision. | Give the **Listen to a Kahani** section an immediately playable approved excerpt. Pinna's subscription offer is not the model for Hey Kahani. |

## Decisions to settle before homepage design

1. Confirm the Founder's Bundle contents and final naming: the SOW specifies **Player + Daily Life Koel + Festivals Koel + 20 stories**; current public copy says **20 Koels**.
2. Confirm which approved story samples and Koel artwork are ready to feature. The homepage should not tease unpublished stories as available purchases.
3. Clarify whether the parent app is required for initial device setup, then phrase “screen-free” around the child's listening experience accurately.
4. Confirm which real testimonials/customer assets are approved. The SOW does not permit invented reviews or ratings.
